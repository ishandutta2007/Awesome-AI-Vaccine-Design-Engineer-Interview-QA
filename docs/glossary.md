# Glossary: AI Vaccine Design Terminology

## Terms A–M

**Adjuvant** – Substance co-administered with an antigen to enhance the resulting immune response, typically via innate immune pathway activation.

**Antigenic drift** – Gradual accumulation of point mutations in a pathogen's surface antigens over time, eroding population immunity.

**Antigenic shift** – Abrupt, large genetic change (e.g., via reassortment) introducing a novel antigenic subtype with little pre-existing population immunity.

**B-cell epitope** – Region of an antigen recognized by B-cell receptors/antibodies, either linear (contiguous sequence) or conformational (structurally assembled from distant sequence regions).

**Conformational epitope** – B-cell epitope formed by amino acid residues from different parts of a folded protein's primary sequence, brought into spatial proximity by 3D structure.

**Correlate of protection (CoP)** – Measurable immune response statistically/mechanistically associated with protection, usable as a surrogate endpoint for vaccine efficacy evaluation.

**Deep mutational scanning** – Experimental method systematically measuring the functional/fitness consequences of comprehensive point mutations across a protein.

**Epitope scaffolding** – Structure-based immunogen design technique presenting an isolated epitope on an engineered structural scaffold outside its native protein context.

**HLA (Human Leukocyte Antigen)** – Human genes encoding MHC molecules; highly polymorphic, central to T-cell epitope presentation.

**Immunogenicity** – The capacity of an antigen to provoke an immune response.

**MHC (Major Histocompatibility Complex)** – Cell-surface molecules presenting processed peptide antigens to T cells; class I (CD8+ T cells) and class II (CD4+ T cells).

**Molecular dynamics (MD)** – Computational simulation method modeling the time-evolution of atomic positions in a molecular structure.

## Terms N–Z

**Population coverage** – The proportion of a target population predicted to mount an immune response to a given set of epitopes, given HLA allele frequency distribution.

**Prefusion stabilization** – Structure-based engineering strategy (e.g., "2P"/"HexaPro" proline substitutions) locking a viral fusion protein in its prefusion conformation to preserve neutralization-sensitive epitopes.

**Prototype pathogen approach** – Pandemic preparedness strategy developing vaccine design expertise/candidates for representative pathogens from virus families with pandemic potential, before actual emergence.

**Reverse vaccinology** – Genome-driven computational approach to vaccine antigen discovery, screening a pathogen's predicted proteome for candidate antigens rather than starting from cultured pathogen material.

**T-cell epitope** – Short peptide fragment (bound to MHC) recognized by T-cell receptors.

**TAP (Transporter associated with Antigen Processing)** – Protein complex transporting proteasome-generated peptides into the endoplasmic reticulum for MHC class I loading.

**Th1/Th2/Th17 polarization** – Distinct T-helper cell response patterns shaping the character of the resulting adaptive immune response.

**Universal vaccine** – Vaccine design strategy targeting broadly conserved, escape-resistant epitopes to achieve protection across multiple strains/variants of a pathogen (e.g., universal influenza, pan-coronavirus).

**VLP (Virus-Like Particle)** – Vaccine platform using self-assembling, non-infectious particles displaying antigen in a multivalent, virus-mimicking structural context.
