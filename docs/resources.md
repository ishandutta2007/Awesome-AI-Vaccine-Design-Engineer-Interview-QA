# Resources: Further Reading & Tools for AI Vaccine Design Engineers

## Key Guidelines & Standards
- **FDA vaccine guidance documents** (fda.gov, CBER — Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)
- **WHO vaccine prequalification and strain selection guidance** (who.int)
- **ICH guidance documents relevant to biologics/vaccine development** (ich.org)

## Landmark Papers
- Rappuoli (2000) — "Reverse vaccinology" (Current Opinion in Microbiology) — foundational reverse vaccinology methodology paper
- McLellan et al. (2013) and Pallesen et al. (2017) — prefusion stabilization ("2P") structural design papers for RSV F and coronavirus spike proteins
- Hie et al. (2021) — "Learning the language of viral evolution and escape" (Science) — ML approaches to escape prediction
- Jumper et al. (2021) — AlphaFold2 foundational structure prediction paper (see also Neural Interface Protocol Designer repo resources)

## Textbooks & References
- **"Vaccines"** (Plotkin, Orenstein, Offit, eds.) — comprehensive standard vaccinology reference textbook
- **"Janeway's Immunobiology"** (Murphy & Weaver) — foundational immunology textbook
- **"Structural Vaccinology"** (Rappuoli & Bagnoli, eds.) — structure-based vaccine design reference

## Organizations
- **CEPI (Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations)** — pandemic preparedness funding/coordination organization
- **WHO** — vaccine prequalification, strain selection coordination
- **IEDB (Immune Epitope Database)** — community epitope data resource and prediction tools (iedb.org)
- **Sabin Vaccine Institute** — vaccine access and development advocacy organization

| Category | Examples |
